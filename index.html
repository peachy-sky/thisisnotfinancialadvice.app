Why Learn Personal Finance Skills?

"It's arguably the most important skill of our lives, and school doesn't teach it. Our society runs on money, so it's high time we learn it!"

In This Is Not Financial Advice; The Money Learning Game, you help townsfolk and play through rounds representing their lives, making "years" of actual financial decisions and learning how to use the personal finance tools of today - Checking and Savings, HYSAs, CDs, stock investing, 401ks, Roth IRAs, real estate, and so much more.

And, at the end of the day, learn that money isn't about the money - it's about the life it impacts, the possibilities it opens up, and the dreams it helps build. Gain confidence in your own money skills, so you can make the system work for you and achieve your own dreams in life. After all, financial freedom is one of the most important freedoms out there.

Hi, I'm Chloe.

Heyyo! My name is Chloe, and I am the twentysomething sidequester behind This Is Not Financial Advice. I have always been passionate about financial freedom (ever since my first "business" in 4th grade!), and will talk your ear off over it for hours if you let me.

I believe that financial freedom is not about the money - it's not about the math, and it's not about the numbers. Ultimately, financial freedom is about the human life it impacts, the doors it opens, and the human being that it empowers - so that that person can live their life to the fullest (whatever that may mean to them!).

Over the years, I had been shocked and a little horrified to learn just how few people were taught personal financial education in school. After graduating, I worked at a bank, where parents told me daily how much they wished their kids could get some better financial education. I try to never let a friend of mine enter adult life without some good money basics, so it's a dream to finally be building something to help share those good money basics with younger students. I want to inspire kids to get excited about personal finance - really excited - and strive for the lives they can lead when money isn't a worry but a point of pride.
